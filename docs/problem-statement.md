# Problem statement: Clinic Queue

**Course:** CEF 345, Group 1
**Project:** clinic-queue (clinic appointments and queue)
**Written by:** Analysis team (Nyietalem, Ticha)
**Date:** 10/11/2026

## The client

The client is a small private clinic in Buea that receives many patients
every day (several dozen) for general consultation, lab tests and pharmacy.
It has one receptionist who also acts as cashier, two to three nurses, one or
two general practitioners and one lab technician.

Today the clinic has no digital system. A patient who arrives writes their
name in a paper notebook at reception, and the receptionist calls the names
aloud. After the consultation, the patient joins another manual queue at the
lab or the pharmacy.

## Problems today

1. **Long and unpredictable waiting time.** Patients wait a very long time in
   a crowded waiting room without knowing their position in the queue. They
   cannot leave to buy water or go to the pharmacy because they fear missing
   their turn. Elderly and sick patients suffer the most.
2. **Queue jumping and conflicts.** The queue is on paper and names are called
   aloud, so patients argue about who arrived first. Friends or relatives of
   the staff are sometimes called earlier, which causes conflict at reception
   and a loss of trust in the clinic.
3. **No record of the day.** The notebook is often lost, torn or hard to read.
   The clinic cannot tell how many patients waited, for how long, or how many
   patients each doctor saw.
4. **Patients who leave without being seen.** When the waiting room is full,
   some patients leave without telling reception. Their names are still
   called later, and the doctor waits for a patient who is no longer there.
5. **No appointment times, so everyone arrives at once.** Patients cannot book
   a time, so they all arrive at the same moment. A doctor is then sometimes
   given several patients at once.

## Sources

- Observation: visit to a private clinic in Buea.
- Informal interview: the receptionist and two patients waiting in the queue.
